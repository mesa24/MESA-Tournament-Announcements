# MPS: Summer 2026 - Official Tournament Announcement

**Tournament Operator:** MESA  
**Announcement Date:** July 7, 2026

---

## Tournament Dates

**Open Qualifier:** July 21, 2026 (Online)  
**Main Event:** July 23-25, 2026 (Online)

---

## Additional Information

### Direct VRS Invites

**None.** This tournament does not feature any Direct VRS Invites or Wildcard Invites.

### VRS Information for Seeding

- **Seeding VRS Publication Date:** Latest available version at time of registration close
- VRS-ranked rosters will be seeded higher than non-VRS rosters in the Open Qualifier
- Among VRS-ranked teams, seeding will follow their Global VRS ranking

### Visa Region

Mongolia

### Tournament Stages

- **Open Qualifier:** Online
- **Playoffs:** Online

### Prize Pool

**Total Prize Pool:** 10,000,000 MNT

**Distribution:**

- 1st Place: 5,000,000 MNT
- 2nd Place: 3,000,000 MNT
- 3rd-4th Place: 1,000,000 MNT

**Additional Compensation:** None. No appearance fees, revenue shares, or other forms of compensation beyond the prize pool.

### Open Qualifier Rules

- **Registration:** mesa.mn
- **Registration Fee:** 50,000 MNT (Mongolian bank transfer)
- **Registration Period:** July 14 - July 19, 2026
- **No team limit**
- **Advancement:** Top 4 teams qualify for Main Event

### Open Qualifier Format

**Format:** Single-Elimination bracket, all matches Best of 3 (Bo3).

Bracket size will scale to the number of registered teams at Registration close. Seeding will follow Global VRS ranking where available; unranked rosters will be seeded below all VRS-ranked rosters and ordered by registration timestamp among themselves.

If the number of registered teams is not a power of 2, top-seeded teams will receive byes into the second round to fill out the bracket.

### Main Event Format

**Playoffs (Online):**

- Double-Elimination bracket
- All matches: Bo3

### Integrity and Conduct Rules

All participants must comply with the **MESA CS General Rulebook** (Sections 56-71).

**Disqualification criteria include:**

- Cheating or prohibited software use
- Match fixing
- Doping violations
- Betting on tournament matches
- Competition manipulation
- Code of Conduct violations

These rules are transparent, objective, and applied equally to all participants. Full details available in the MESA CS General Rulebook.
